"# CalMatrixOffline-semifinal-261022" 
Food/exercise calories offline management app
Your food data stays on your phone. Not on a server. Not in the cloud. Not shared with anyone. Ever.

CalMatrix Offline is a fully offline calorie and nutrition tracker that works anywhere — on a plane, in a subway, off-grid. No internet. No account. No subscription.

🔥 WHAT YOU GET:

✅ Search 1000+ foods instantly (cheeseburger, chicken, apple, etc.)
✅ Log exercises with weight + duration (jogging, cycling, swimming, etc.)
✅ See your daily calorie balance (food - exercise)
✅ Track weight over time
✅ View 7-day trends chart
✅ Get daily insights & warnings
✅ Export your data to CSV (with timestamp)
✅ Import your own food/exercise/barcode data via CSV
✅ Barcode scanner for packaged foods
✅ 100% offline — no internet required
✅ 100% private — no data sent anywhere
✅ No subscription — pay once, use forever
